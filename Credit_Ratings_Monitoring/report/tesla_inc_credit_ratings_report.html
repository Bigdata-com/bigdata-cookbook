Tesla Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-01-01

Credit Report

  • 2021-10-22: S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla’s issuer credit rating to BB+ and assigned a positive outlook, citing strong demand, expected delivery and earnings growth, robust financial metrics, and increasingly consistent positive free operating cash flow despite chip shortages and other supply constraints (MT Newswires).
  • 2022-01-25: Moody’s upgraded Tesla’s corporate family/debt rating two notches to Ba1 from Ba3 and assigned a positive outlook, reflecting expectations for expanding global EV production, approximately 1.4 million 2022 deliveries, strong profitability and cash-flow growth, and very good liquidity supported by substantial cash balances (Moody's, Benzinga).
  • 2022-10-06: S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla’s issuer and issue-level ratings two notches to BBB from BB+, moving the company into investment-grade territory, and assigned a stable outlook based on higher production and deliveries, global capacity expansion, strong margins, positive free cash flow, low leverage, profitability, and ample liquidity (Morningstar, Investing.com, Bloomberg News).
  • 2023-03-20: Moody’s upgraded Tesla’s long-term issuer rating to Baa3 from Ba1, withdrew the former Ba1 corporate family, Ba1-PD probability-of-default, and SGL-1 liquidity ratings, and maintained a stable outlook, citing Tesla’s leading global EV position, expanding product range and manufacturing footprint, very high profitability, more than $7 billion in expected free cash flow, limited debt, and leverage below 1x (The Fly, BNN Bloomberg, Benzinga, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).

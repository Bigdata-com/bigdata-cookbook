Tesla Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-01-01

Credit Report

  • 2021-10-22: On October 22, 2021, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s issuer credit rating to 'BB+' with a positive outlook, reflecting expectations of consistent positive free operating cash flow generation amid global manufacturing expansion. This upgrade is supported by solid demand prospects and robust financial metrics (The Fly, MT Newswires).
  • 2021-11-10: On November 10, 2021, Moody's downgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating further into junk status due to production shortfalls and regulatory scrutiny, exacerbated by a controversial tweet from Elon Musk. The downgrade contributed to a significant decline in Tesla's stock (Gulf Business).
  • 2022-01-25: On January 25, 2022, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s debt rating from Ba3 to Ba1, indicating a move closer to investment grade, driven by a bullish outlook on Tesla's market position in electric vehicles. This upgrade raised expectations for potential further upgrades contingent on performance maintenance (Benzinga, Bloomberg News).
  • 2022-10-06: On October 6, 2022, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating from BB+ to BBB, reflecting higher-than-expected deliveries and production ramp-up, with a stable outlook. This upgrade is expected to lower Tesla's borrowing costs (Morningstar, Investing.com via Web, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2023-03-20: On March 20, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3 and withdrew previous ratings, reflecting expectations of continued leadership in battery electric vehicles and high profitability. The outlook is stable, supported by governance factors and operational track record (The Fly).
  • 2023-03-21: On March 21, 2023, Tesla Inc. was recognized for its upgrade to investment-grade status by Moody's, moving from Ba1 to Baa3, attributed to expanding product offerings and improved financial leverage. The stock rose over 7% following the announcement, buoyed by strong sales data from China (BNN Bloomberg, Morningstar, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2023-03-22: On March 22, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3, marking its exit from speculative junk status and enhancing its appeal to risk-averse investors. The upgrade reflects Tesla's dominant market position and improved capital discipline (Investing.com via Web, Business Insider).
  • 2023-03-24: On March 24, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to investment grade, contributing to a solid rise in Tesla's stock amid increasing registrations in China for the electric vehicle manufacturer (Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2024-05-02: On May 2, 2024, S&P affirmed Tesla Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB', despite governance concerns related to CEO Elon Musk's influence. The agency expects strong EBITDA margins over the next two years, even amid price cuts to maintain market share (Benzinga).
  • 2024-10-16: On October 16, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned ratings to the Tesla Auto Lease Trust 2024-B, indicating adequate credit enhancement and expected ratings of 'AAAsf', 'AAsf', and 'Asf'. The ratings were solicited and assigned at the request of the rated entity (Fitch Ratings).
  • 2024-10-28: On October 28, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned expected ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, reflecting compliance with EU and UK regulatory frameworks. The ratings were solicited and assigned at the request of the rated entity (Fitch Ratings).
  • 2024-11-12: On November 12, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned final ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, with a credit rating of AAAsf, compliant with EU and UK regulations. The ratings were solicited and assigned at the request of the rated entity (Fitch Ratings).

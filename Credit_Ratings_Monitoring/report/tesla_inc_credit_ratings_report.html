Credit Report

  • 2026-06-03: On June 3, 2026, Fitch Ratings assigned final ratings to four classes of notes issued by Tesla Sustainable Energy Business Trust 2026-1, marking a new rating for the securitization of consumer loans backed by residential solar equipment. Key drivers included Tesla's established market presence and strong borrower profiles, despite a shorter loan performance history (Fitch Ratings).
  • 2026-07-10: On July 10, 2026, Fitch Ratings affirmed the ratings for Tesla Auto Lease Trust 2024-A and 2024-B, and upgraded the ratings for class C notes of TALT 2024-B and class B notes of TLEVS 2025-A, with a Stable Outlook assigned to TALT 2024-B class C notes and a Positive Outlook for TLEVS 2025-A class B notes. The TLEVS 2025-A class C notes' outlook was revised to Positive from Stable, reflecting improved performance expectations (Fitch Ratings).

🔍 Key Findings

Winner: Eli Lilly emerges as the clear winner with $50B in US investments since 2020, 9 domestic plants, and CEO alignment with administration policies.
Strong Positions: Johnson & Johnson ($55B commitment) and AbbVie (balanced 9:9 US/foreign ratio) are well-positioned to benefit from exemptions.
Prepared: Merck has strategically invested $12B+ and opened key facilities in Delaware and North Carolina, ensuring exemption eligibility.
Cautious: Pfizer, despite having 11 US sites, shows investment hesitation due to policy uncertainty, potentially missing opportunities.
Common Strategy: All companies are qualifying for exemptions through active construction projects, with combined investments exceeding $150B.
Market Impact: The tariff policy is driving the largest pharmaceutical manufacturing reshoring in US history, fundamentally altering global supply chains.

Supply Chain Reshaping — Thematic Exposure Screen¶

Preferred approach: For production workflows, use Thematic_Screener_CLI (CLI, MCP, derivative modes, full artifact export). This notebook is a simplified walkthrough that delegates to the same REST pipeline. See Thematic Screeners on docs.bigdata.com.

Geopolitical tension, reshoring incentives, and logistics bottlenecks are reshaping where companies source, manufacture, and distribute. The question for a portfolio manager is not whether supply chains matter — it is which holdings are most exposed, through which channels, and with what evidence in primary documents.

This notebook screens the top 100 NASDAQ companies by market cap for exposure to Supply Chain Reshaping. It:

  1. Builds a mindmap of exposure pathways for the theme
  2. Searches filings, earnings calls, and premium news across the universe
  3. Classifies every retrieved passage and attributes it to a company and pathway
  4. Summarises each exposed company and exports the evidence pack

Every conclusion traces back to a dated, attributable quote from a primary document.

The pipeline logic lives in Thematic_Screener_CLI (src package); this notebook only orchestrates and presents it. Two credentials are read from the project .env file: BIGDATA_API_KEY for evidence retrieval and OPENAI_API_KEY for taxonomy generation and classification.

Defining the screen¶

Four inputs define the analysis:

Input Purpose
Theme The investment theme whose exposure we map
Analyst focus Steers the mindmap toward the most relevant sub-themes
Universe Top 100 NASDAQ listings by market cap
Window The publication period searched for evidence

RETRIEVAL_DEPTH sets how deeply each pathway samples its available evidence pool. A deeper sample yields more quotes per pathway and a longer run; 1% is ample for a screen of this size.

Every artefact is written to an isolated run directory under Thematic_Screener_CLI/runs/, so this analysis can be reproduced, audited, or resumed from the command line later.

Verizon Communications Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-09-01

Credit Report

  • 2023-07-20: On July 20, 2023, Verizon Communications Inc. holds credit ratings of BBB+ from S&P, Baa1 from Moody's, and A- from Fitch, all with a stable outlook. The company projects a free cash flow of $17 billion this year, sufficient to retire all debt maturing through 2024 and half of its remaining floating rate debt (Forbes.com).
  • 2023-10-09: On October 9, 2023, Verizon's Baa1 credit rating from Moody's indicates a moderate risk level, with a Total Debt to Equity Ratio of 188.91%, suggesting higher risk for investors, leading to a recommendation to underweight Verizon in investment portfolios (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2023-10-18: On October 18, 2023, Moody's affirmed Verizon's Baa1 credit rating and Prime-2 rating for domestic commercial paper, maintaining a stable outlook despite a 3.5% year-over-year revenue decline in Q2 FY23, primarily due to a significant drop in wireless equipment sales (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-01-12: On January 12, 2024, Verizon holds a credit rating of BBB+/stable from S&P, with positive growth in its 5G broadband service, achieving over 400,000 net additions each quarter, which is expected to enhance customer retention and margins (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-03-14: On March 14, 2024, Verizon is reported to have a Baa1 credit rating from Moody's, indicating moderate credit risk, with comparisons to other companies in a dividend portfolio (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-09-10: On September 10, 2024, Verizon's credit rating is reported as BBB+ from S&P Global and A- from Fitch, with an anticipated leverage ratio of approximately 3.0x following the acquisition of Frontier, remaining below the downgrade threshold, while the credit outlook stays stable (Nasdaq).

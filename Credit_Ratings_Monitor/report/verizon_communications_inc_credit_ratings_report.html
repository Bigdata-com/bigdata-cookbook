Verizon Communications Inc.

2021-10-01 - 2024-11-12

Credit Report

  • 2023-07-20: Verizon Communications Inc. holds credit ratings of BBB+ from S&P, Baa1 from Moody's, and A- from Fitch, all with a stable outlook. The company projects a free cash flow of \$17 billion for the year, sufficient to retire all debt maturing through 2024, indicating no immediate downgrade pressure (Forbes.com).
  • 2023-10-18: Moody's affirmed Verizon's Baa1 credit rating for its unsecured domestic notes and Prime-2 rating for commercial paper, maintaining a stable outlook. The firm reported a year-over-year revenue decline of approximately 3.5% in Q2 FY23, primarily due to a significant drop in wireless equipment sales, impacting cash flows and the balance sheet (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-09-10: Verizon's credit ratings are reported as BBB+ from S&P Global and A- from Fitch, reflecting strong investment-grade status. Following the acquisition of Frontier, S&P anticipates an increase in Verizon's leverage ratio to approximately 3.0x, which remains below the downgrade threshold, with a stable credit outlook (Nasdaq).

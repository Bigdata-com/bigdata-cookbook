Tesla Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-01-01

Credit Report

  • 2021-10-22: On October 22, 2021, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s issuer credit rating to 'BB+' with a positive outlook, reflecting expectations of consistent positive cash flow generation despite industry supply bottlenecks. This upgrade is supported by solid demand prospects and robust financial metrics (The Fly, MT Newswires).
  • 2021-11-10: On November 10, 2021, Moody's Investors Service downgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating further into junk status due to production shortfalls and regulatory scrutiny over its Autopilot system, exacerbated by Elon Musk's controversial statements. This downgrade contributed to a 3.7% decline in Tesla's stock (Gulf Business).
  • 2022-01-26: On January 26, 2022, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Ba1, the highest junk rating, due to improving profitability and business-scale prospects, positioning Tesla close to investment grade (Business Standard via Web).
  • 2022-10-06: On October 6, 2022, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating from BB+ to BBB, reflecting improved production and solid cash flow prospects, with a stable outlook. This upgrade is expected to lower borrowing costs for Tesla (Morningstar, Investing.com).
  • 2023-03-20: On March 20, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3 and withdrew its previous ratings, reflecting expectations of continued leadership in battery electric vehicles and stable outlook due to prudent financial policies. This upgrade marks a significant improvement in Tesla's credit quality (The Fly).
  • 2023-03-21: On March 21, 2023, Tesla Inc. was recognized with investment-grade status by Moody's, following a similar upgrade by S&P in October 2022, attributed to expanding product offerings and improved financial leverage. The stock reacted positively, rising over 7% on the day (BNN Bloomberg, Morningstar, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2024-05-02: On May 2, 2024, S&P affirmed Tesla Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB', citing solid market share and liquidity despite governance concerns related to CEO Elon Musk. The affirmation reflects expectations of strong EBITDA margins and improvements in manufacturing efficiency (Benzinga).
  • 2024-11-12: On November 12, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned final ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, with a credit rating of AAAsf, compliant with EU and UK regulations. This rating reflects the trust's strong financial backing and regulatory endorsement (Fitch Ratings).

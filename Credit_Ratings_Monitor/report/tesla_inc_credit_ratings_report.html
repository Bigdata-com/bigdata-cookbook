Tesla Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-01-01

Credit Report

  • 2021-10-22: On October 22, 2021, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s issuer credit rating to 'BB+' with a positive outlook, reflecting expectations of consistent positive free operating cash flow generation despite near-term supply bottlenecks. This upgrade is supported by solid demand prospects and robust financial metrics (The Fly, MT Newswires).
  • 2021-11-09: On November 9, 2021, Moody's Investors Service downgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating further into junk status due to production shortfalls and regulatory issues, exacerbated by Elon Musk's controversial tweets. This downgrade contributed to a volatile stock trajectory, leading to a notable decline in share value (Bloomberg News).
  • 2022-01-25: On January 25, 2022, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s debt rating by two notches from Ba3 to Ba1, indicating a move closer to investment grade status, driven by expectations of maintaining its leading position in the electric vehicle market (Benzinga, Bloomberg News).
  • 2022-10-06: On October 6, 2022, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating from BB+ to BBB, marking a significant move out of junk territory, supported by strong production capabilities and solid cash flow prospects. The outlook is now stable, reflecting Tesla's market leadership in electric vehicles (Morningstar, Investing.com).
  • 2023-03-20: On March 20, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3 and withdrew previous ratings, reflecting expectations of continued leadership in battery electric vehicles and high profitability. The outlook is stable, considering governance factors and operational track record (The Fly).
  • 2023-03-21: On March 21, 2023, Tesla Inc. was recognized with an investment-grade rating by two major credit rating agencies following Moody's upgrade from Ba1 to Baa3. This upgrade was driven by improved financial leverage and strong retail sales data from China, contributing to a 7.0% surge in Tesla's stock (BNN Bloomberg, Morningstar).
  • 2023-03-22: On March 22, 2023, Tesla Inc. received a credit rating upgrade from Moody's, moving from Ba1 to Baa3, thus exiting speculative junk status. This upgrade enhances Tesla's appeal to risk-averse investors and reflects its dominant market position and improved capital discipline (Investing.com via Web, Business Insider).
  • 2023-03-27: On March 27, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3, marking a significant improvement in credit quality. This upgrade follows a similar upgrade by S&P Global and is supported by strong delivery performance and market expansion efforts (Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2024-05-02: On May 2, 2024, S&P affirmed Tesla Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB', citing solid market share and liquidity despite governance concerns regarding CEO Elon Musk's influence. The affirmation reflects expectations of strong EBITDA margins amid price cuts to maintain market share (Benzinga).
  • 2024-11-12: On November 12, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned final ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, with a credit rating of AAAsf, indicating compliance with EU and UK regulatory frameworks. No changes in credit outlook or watchlist status were reported (Fitch Ratings).

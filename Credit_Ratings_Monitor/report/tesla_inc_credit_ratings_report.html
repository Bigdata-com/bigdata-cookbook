Tesla Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-09-01

Credit Report

  • 2021-10-22: On October 22, 2021, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s issuer credit rating to 'BB+' with a positive outlook, reflecting solid demand prospects and robust financial metrics, despite near-term supply bottlenecks (The Fly, MT Newswires).
  • 2021-11-09: On November 9, 2021, Moody's downgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating, pushing it further into junk status due to production shortfalls and regulatory issues, compounded by controversial statements from CEO Elon Musk that affected stock performance (Bloomberg News).
  • 2022-01-25: On January 25, 2022, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s debt rating from Ba3 to Ba1, indicating a move closer to investment grade, driven by expectations of maintaining its leading position in the electric vehicle market (Benzinga, Bloomberg News).
  • 2022-10-06: On October 6, 2022, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating from BB+ to BBB, reflecting higher-than-expected deliveries and strong demand, with a stable outlook (Morningstar, Investing.com, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2023-03-20: On March 20, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3 and withdrew its previous ratings, reflecting expectations of continued leadership in the electric vehicle market and stable outlook due to strong profitability and governance factors (The Fly).
  • 2023-03-21: On March 21, 2023, Tesla Inc. was recognized with investment-grade status by Moody's, following a similar upgrade by S&P in October 2022, attributed to expanding product offerings and improved financial leverage (BNN Bloomberg, Morningstar, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2023-03-22: On March 22, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3, marking its exit from speculative junk status, driven by strong market position and improved capital discipline (Investing.com via Web, Business Insider, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2024-05-02: On May 2, 2024, S&P affirmed Tesla Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB', citing solid market share and liquidity, despite governance concerns related to CEO Elon Musk's influence (Benzinga).
  • 2024-10-28: On October 28, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned expected ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, indicating compliance with EU and UK regulations (Fitch Ratings).
  • 2024-11-12: On November 12, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned final ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, maintaining a credit rating of AAAsf, with no changes reported (Fitch Ratings).

Tesla Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-01-01

Credit Report

  • 2021-10-22: On October 22, 2021, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s issuer credit rating to 'BB+' with a positive outlook, reflecting expectations of consistent positive cash flow generation amid global manufacturing expansion. This upgrade is supported by strong demand prospects and robust financial metrics (The Fly, MT Newswires).
  • 2021-11-10: On November 10, 2021, Moody's downgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating further into junk status due to production shortfalls and regulatory scrutiny over its Autopilot system, contributing to a decline in Tesla's stock (Gulf Business).
  • 2022-01-25: On January 25, 2022, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s debt rating from Ba3 to Ba1, indicating a move closer to investment grade, reflecting a bullish outlook on Tesla's market position and profitability (Benzinga, Bloomberg News).
  • 2022-10-06: On October 6, 2022, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating from BB+ to BBB, marking a significant move out of junk territory, driven by strong production capabilities and solid cash flow prospects (Morningstar, Investing.com via Web, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2023-03-20: On March 20, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to Baa3 and withdrew its previous Ba1 rating, reflecting expectations of continued leadership in the electric vehicle market and stable outlook due to prudent financial policies (The Fly).
  • 2023-03-21: On March 21, 2023, Tesla Inc. was recognized with investment-grade status by Moody's, following a similar upgrade by S&P in October 2022, attributed to expanding product offerings and improved financial leverage (BNN Bloomberg, Morningstar, Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2023-03-24: On March 24, 2023, Moody's upgraded Tesla Inc.'s credit rating to investment grade, contributing to a solid rise in Tesla's stock amid increasing registrations in China for the electric vehicle manufacturer (Investor's Business Daily (IBD)).
  • 2024-05-02: On May 2, 2024, S&P affirmed Tesla Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB', citing solid market share and liquidity despite governance concerns related to CEO Elon Musk's influence. The agency expects improvements in manufacturing efficiency to mitigate declines in EBITDA margin (Benzinga).
  • 2024-11-12: On November 12, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned final ratings to the Tesla Sustainable Energy Trust 2024-1, with a credit rating of AAAsf, compliant with EU and UK regulations, indicating strong financial management (Fitch Ratings).

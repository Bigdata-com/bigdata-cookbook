Bayer AG

2021-09-01 - 2025-09-01

Credit Report

  • 2023-10-27: On October 27, 2023, Bayer AG holds an investment-grade credit rating of BBB+ from Fitch and BBB from S&P Global, despite challenges such as litigation suits and a decline in its crop science department; however, revenues have been increasing since 2017, and pressures are expected to be temporary (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-09-25: On September 25, 2024, Fitch Ratings assigned a credit rating of 'BB+' to Bayer AG's EUR750 million subordinated notes, reflecting an expected EBITDA margin decrease to 20.5% for 2024 due to lower margins in the pharma and crop science sectors, with a projected recovery to 22% by 2027 (Fitch Ratings).

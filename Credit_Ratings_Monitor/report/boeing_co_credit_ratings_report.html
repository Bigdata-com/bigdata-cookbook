Boeing Co.

2021-10-01 - 2024-11-12

Credit Report

  • 2024-03-26: Moody's placed Boeing Co.'s Baa2 senior unsecured rating and Prime-2 short-term rating on review for downgrade due to concerns over cash flow generation and production challenges, particularly with the 737 aircraft. This review reflects the financial impact of ongoing safety issues and leadership changes, including the departure of CEO Dave Calhoun (BNN Bloomberg, Investing.com, NBC Philadelphia).
  • 2024-04-24: Moody's downgraded Boeing Co.'s credit rating to Baa3 from Baa2, citing concerns over cash flow generation and the company's ability to manage over \$12 billion in debt maturing in 2025 and 2026. The outlook is currently negative, reflecting ongoing financial strain despite a shallower loss per share than initially feared (Zero Hedge, MT Newswires).
  • 2024-04-25: Boeing faced further credit rating pressures as Moody's downgraded its rating to Baa2, just above junk status, with a negative outlook. This downgrade followed a report of a 7.5% revenue decline and significant losses in its commercial airliner business, exacerbated by production issues (Atlanta Voice, Finance News Network, Business Standard).
  • 2024-04-26: Moody's downgraded Boeing's credit rating to one notch above junk status, citing a \$1.14 billion loss in its commercial airliner business and a 7.5% revenue decline. Concurrently, S&P affirmed Boeing's long-term credit rating at 'BBB-' but revised its outlook from 'stable' to 'negative' due to increased risks of cash flow recovery delays (ShareCafe, Yahoo! Finance).
  • 2024-05-01: Fitch Ratings downgraded Boeing's credit outlook from Stable to Negative due to ongoing production and cash flow issues, indicating a moderate risk of default. S&P and Moody's rated Boeing's debt just above junk status, with potential implications for future borrowing costs (InvestorPlace).
  • 2024-09-13: Boeing faced significant challenges due to a machinist strike, prompting Fitch Ratings to warn of a potential downgrade of its investment-grade credit rating. Moody's placed all of Boeing's credit ratings on review for a possible downgrade, reflecting concerns about cash flow and operational disruptions (Yahoo! Finance, Fitch Ratings, Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-10-01: Boeing is considering issuing \$10 billion in new shares to address cash needs amid an ongoing strike that poses significant risks to its credit rating. Both Moody's and Fitch indicated that the strike could jeopardize Boeing's investment-grade status, with analysts predicting substantial cash outflows (Morningstar, BNN Bloomberg).
  • 2024-10-08: S&P Global placed Boeing on CreditWatch Negative due to ongoing labor strikes significantly impacting its financial stability. The agency indicated that the strike is costing the company over \$1 billion per month, raising concerns about Boeing's ability to meet debt obligations (Seeking Alpha, Benzinga).
  • 2024-10-29: Boeing is actively seeking to raise up to \$24.3 billion through a stock offering to improve its financial position amid ongoing strike actions. Credit rating agencies are considering downgrading Boeing's credit rating from its current investment-grade status, with S&P warning of a potential downgrade if cash balances fall below \$10 billion (Farnborough International News Network, Business Standard).
  • 2024-11-05: Following a seven-week strike costing approximately \$100 million per day, Boeing raised \$24 billion from investors to mitigate losses and maintain its investment-grade credit rating. However, S&P Global Ratings has placed Boeing on a negative credit watch, indicating that the company's credit rating is still under review for potential downgrade (CEO Today, Oman Daily Observer).

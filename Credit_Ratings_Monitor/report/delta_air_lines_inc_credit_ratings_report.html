Delta Air Lines Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-09-01

Credit Report

  • 2023-10-13: Delta Air Lines Inc. has made significant strides in reducing its debt, paying down $3.7 billion year-to-date, and is on track to regain its investment-grade status, as recognized by S&P. The company reported record quarterly results with net revenue of $15.49 billion, driven by strong performance across all segments (Nasdaq).
  • 2023-12-13: Delta Air Lines Inc. received a credit rating improvement from S&P, attributed to effective cost management and significant debt repayment, with an operating income of $2.0 billion and a solid cash flow of $1.1 billion. The upgrade reflects Delta's healthy profitability and operational performance (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-02-09: Delta Air Lines Inc. holds a credit rating of BB+ with a positive outlook according to S&P Global Ratings, positioning it favorably among major US airlines. The analysis indicates Delta's strong position compared to peers, with optimistic future guidance (AeroTime.aero).
  • 2024-06-14: Delta Air Lines Inc. retained its investment-grade rating at Moody's but is rated one notch below investment-grade by Fitch and S&P, both with a positive outlook. The airline is actively working to regain its investment-grade ratings, having repaid $12 billion of debt and showing optimism for an upgrade later in the year (Livemint).
  • 2024-07-01: Fitch Ratings affirmed Delta Air Lines Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB' with a positive outlook, indicating stable credit quality supported by strong cash flow generation. This affirmation reflects confidence in Delta's operational performance and market position (Fitch Ratings).
  • 2024-07-16: Fitch Ratings upgraded Delta Air Lines Inc.'s credit rating to 'BBB-' and restored its investment-grade status, citing significant improvements in the company's balance sheet and capital spending aligned with public guidance. This marks a significant recovery from the previous junk rating assigned during the pandemic (Fitch Ratings, Fitch Ratings, Investing.com via Web).
  • 2024-07-19: Delta Air Lines Inc. received an upgrade in its credit rating from Fitch Ratings, moving from 'BB+' to 'BBB-', reflecting significant improvements in financial health and debt reduction efforts. This upgrade signifies Delta's regained investment-grade status, attributed to strong performance and positive demand for air travel (Investing.com via Web, Yahoo! Finance).
  • 2024-10-10: Delta Air Lines Inc. received an upgrade to investment grade credit rating by Fitch, recognizing its continued progression on debt reduction and strong financial performance. The company forecasts revenue growth of 2-4% for the upcoming quarter, supported by favorable market conditions (Associated Press, Charlotte Observer).
  • 2024-12-04: S&P Global Ratings upgraded Delta Air Lines Inc. to 'BBB-' from 'BB+' with a stable outlook, reflecting expectations of stronger credit measures due to steady margin expansion and increasing revenue. The upgrade indicates Delta's commitment to strengthening its balance sheet through debt reduction (Nasdaq).
  • 2024-12-05: Delta Air Lines Inc. achieved a significant milestone by having its credit rating upgraded to investment grade by S&P Global Ratings, the last of the three major agencies to do so. This upgrade reflects Delta's strong financial outlook and ongoing efforts to strengthen its balance sheet (MarketScreener via Web).
  • 2025-01-08: Fitch Ratings reinstated Delta Air Lines Inc.'s investment-grade credit rating, citing its best-in-class market share and competitive advantage over smaller airlines. This reinstatement underscores Delta's strong position in the U.S. market (Yahoo! Finance).
  • 2025-02-05: Moody's upgraded Delta Air Lines Inc.'s credit rating to its highest level in decades, marking the third upgrade in eight months. This reflects Delta's strong performance and ongoing efforts to strengthen its balance sheet, with all agencies now rating Delta's credit as investment grade (MarketScreener via Web).
  • 2025-07-08: Delta Air Lines Inc. received two credit rating upgrades since December 2024 from S&P Global Ratings and Moody's, attributed to its strong balance sheet and declining debt balance. Delta now holds investment-grade ratings from all three major rating agencies (Forbes.com via Web).

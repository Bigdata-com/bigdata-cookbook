Enbridge Inc.

2021-09-01 - 2025-09-01

Credit Report

  • 2023-09-06: On September 6, 2023, S&P Global Ratings revised its outlook on Enbridge Inc. to negative from stable while affirming its BBB+ issuer credit rating, following concerns about the company's debt levels due to a $14 billion acquisition of natural gas utilities from Dominion Energy Inc. Moody's also cut its outlook on Enbridge, indicating limited capacity for the company to manage its funding plan without increasing its debt load (MT Newswires, Investing.com via Web).
  • 2023-09-08: On September 8, 2023, Moody's changed Enbridge Inc.'s credit outlook from stable to negative due to the acquisition of US gas utilities, reflecting expectations of continued financial strain. This outlook change is driven by concerns over cash flow generation and overall capital structure (Smart Energy International).
  • 2023-10-28: On October 28, 2023, Enbridge Inc. maintained a BBB+ credit rating from S&P with a stable outlook, despite the near-term strain from the Dominion acquisition. The company's healthy balance sheet and a debt-to-capital ratio of 0.5 were highlighted, alongside a 7.8% year-over-year increase in adjusted EBITDA (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-01-19: On January 19, 2024, Enbridge Inc. held a BBB+ credit rating from S&P Global, with an improved debt to EBITDA ratio of 4.1x, indicating effective balance sheet management. The company's diversified business model and recent acquisitions are expected to enhance gas supply and contribute to growth (Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-06-19: On June 19, 2024, S&P Global Ratings upgraded Enbridge Inc.'s outlook to stable from negative and affirmed its issuer credit rating at BBB+. This reflects S&P's view that execution risk related to acquisitions has been mitigated, with credit metrics aligning with expectations (MT Newswires).
  • 2024-08-02: On August 2, 2024, Enbridge Inc. received positive credit rating actions from multiple agencies, including a DBRS upgrade to A low and the removal of the negative outlook by S&P, affirming a BBB+ stable outlook. The agencies noted favorable leverage targets and anticipated EBITDA contributions from newly acquired utilities (Investing.com via Web, Seeking Alpha).
  • 2024-11-14: On November 14, 2024, Enbridge Inc. maintained an investment grade credit rating of BBB+ from S&P Global, supported by a manageable debt to EBITDA ratio and a strong cash flow derived from long-term contracts. The company is engaged in significant capital projects, enhancing its growth potential (Nasdaq).
  • 2025-06-27: On June 27, 2025, Fitch Ratings affirmed Enbridge Inc.'s credit rating at 'BBB+' with a stable outlook, supported by a CAD29 billion capital expenditure plan for upcoming projects. The ratings reflect the continuation of Enbridge's foreign exchange hedging policy (Fitch Ratings).

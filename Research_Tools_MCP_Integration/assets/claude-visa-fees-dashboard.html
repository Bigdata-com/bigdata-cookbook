⚖️ Legal Challenges Expected

Multiple legal experts have indicated that the $100,000 H-1B fee will likely face immediate legal challenges. Critics argue that the executive branch lacks the statutory authority to impose such fees without Congressional approval. The fee is set to expire on September 21, 2026, unless extended.

Key Legal Concerns: Violation of Administrative Procedure Act, exceeding executive authority, potential discrimination claims. [Source]
